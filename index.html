⚠ Hard Limitations — Always Apply
Cannot prove causation (correlation only)
Selection bias: we choose which ratios to test
p < 0.05 has ~5% false positive rate by definition
SETI data is synthesized — no confirmed alien signals
Sample sizes may be too small for publication (N ≥ 30 needed)
Confounds: Fibonacci growth, instrument bias, selection effects
Tolerance choice (±10%) is a free parameter — document yours
This tool supplements peer review; it cannot replace it
Methodology: Seeded Monte Carlo generates random ratio distributions of identical size; z-score and one-tailed p-value calculated from null distribution. 95% CI derived from null spread.
